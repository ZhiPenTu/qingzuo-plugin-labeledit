# Third-party resources

This MIT repository does not relicense its third-party libraries, models or fonts. Frozen backend dependency data and licenses are collected by PyInstaller. Direct and transitive Python dependency versions are pinned in requirements.txt; frontend dependency metadata and integrity are recorded in package-lock.json.

Arimo and Noto Sans SC are distributed with their original SIL Open Font License texts in backend/fonts. PDFium, pypdf, ReportLab, RapidOCR, ONNX Runtime, OpenCV and CPython retain their upstream licenses. OCR model provenance and checksums are maintained by backend/ocr_service.py and docs/ocr-engine.md. PDF engine details are documented in docs/pdf-engine.md.

The SDK is independently distributed from ZhiPenTu/LabelEdit under MIT. Source extraction is recorded in README.md; the original desktop repository retains the prior release assets and history.
